# Daily Water Check

> A small reminder to pause, refill your glass, and look after yourself.

## How to use it

- Decide on a comfortable number of glasses for your day.
- Add a check whenever you finish one.
- Refill before you are thirsty if that works for you.

## A small note to yourself

This is a prompt, not a scorecard—listen to what your body needs.
